Privacy Policy

Last updated · August 2026

What we collect

Smart Expense stores the transactions, budgets, and goals you enter directly on your device. In this prototype, nothing leaves your phone — there is no server and no account required.

How your data is used

Your data is used only to power the app's own features: balances, budgets, insights, and reminders. We never sell your financial information or share it with advertisers.

Screenshots & receipts

When you scan a payment screenshot or receipt, it's processed to detect amount, merchant, and category. Images are not uploaded anywhere in this prototype.

Your controls

You can export or permanently delete all app data at any time from Profile → Data. Deleting data removes it immediately and cannot be undone.

Contact

For questions about this policy, reach out at privacy@smartexpense.app.
